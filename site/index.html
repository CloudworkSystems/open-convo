True Convo

Your AI, on your terms.

True Convo is a personal AI app for the Mac. You stay in the driver's seat: your conversations and memory live on your own computer, and nothing goes to an outside AI service unless you say so.

In development. Not available yet.

What it is

  • A conversation home where you talk things through with an AI that remembers what matters to you.
  • Dictation that works anywhere you can type on your Mac.
  • Tools that fit how you work, not the other way around.

What we promise

  • Local first. Your conversations, memory and dictation stay on your Mac.
  • You approve. Using a cloud AI model, spending money or sharing anything needs your OK first.
  • No outside sign-in required. You won't need a ChatGPT or other account to use it.
  • No ads, and we don't sell your data.
